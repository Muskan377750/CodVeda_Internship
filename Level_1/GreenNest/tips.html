Simple Eco Tips

Small habits that can make a difference.

01

Carry a Reusable Bottle

Avoid single-use plastic bottles by carrying your own reusable bottle.

02

Save Electricity

Switch off lights, fans and electronic devices when they are not being used.

03

Use Public Transport

Walking, cycling and public transportation can help reduce unnecessary emissions.

04

Reduce Food Waste

Plan meals carefully and avoid buying more food than you need.

05

Plant More

Growing plants and trees helps create healthier and greener surroundings.

06

Recycle Properly

Separate recyclable materials from general household waste.
